Graph tagging with ontology formalises domain concepts — clinical entities, model artefacts, regulatory requirements — as a queryable knowledge graph, where relationships are declared deterministically rather than inferred probabilistically. This provides a traceable chain from AI decisions back to governed source data, directly supporting EU AI Act and IEC 62304 auditability obligations. Adoption requires significant ontology-design investment and integration work with existing MLflow, Qualio, and Azure ML artefacts, warranting careful evaluation before commitment.
