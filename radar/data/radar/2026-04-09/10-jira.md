Rejected. At fewer than 20 people the operational overhead — separate user management, licence cost, and synchronisation with GitHub to maintain the traceability chain — is not justified. GitHub Issues + Projects achieves the same planning and traceability outcome within the existing toolset.

Do not use for new work. See **DEC-003**.
