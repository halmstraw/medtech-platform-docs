The Zachman Framework is a 6×6 matrix ontology that organizes enterprise architecture artifacts by stakeholder perspective (Planner, Owner, Designer, Builder, Subcontractor, Enterprise) and subject focus (What, How, Where, Who, When, Why). For a Class IIa regulated medical device platform, it offers a structured vocabulary for mapping design documents, system specifications, and compliance evidence across multiple stakeholder viewpoints — supporting audit-readiness and regulatory traceability. However, it provides structure without process, and its comprehensiveness can impose significant documentation overhead; it should be assessed for fit alongside lighter-weight EA practices already in use.
